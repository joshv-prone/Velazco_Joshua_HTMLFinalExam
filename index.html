Why Formula 1 is Loved Worldwide

Formula 1 (F1) is the highest class of international open-wheel, single-seater motorsport, featuring 22 drivers across 11 teams competing in 24 Grand Prix races around the world each season. Sanctioned by the FIA since 1950 and run commercially by Formula One Group, it blends extreme speeds (up to around 370 km/h) with cutting-edge engineering and race strategy.

In the Philippines, Formula 1 has grown into a massive weekend tradition. From local sim-racing communities to live watch parties during Grand Prix weekends, thousands of Filipino fans gather to support their favorite constructors and drivers.

Top 4 Essential Rules of Formula 1

  1. Points System: Points are awarded to the top 10 finishers in each Grand Prix, with 25 points going to the race winner.
  2. Mandatory Pit Stops: Drivers must use at least two different dry-weather tire compounds during a dry race.
  3. Qualifying Sessions: Qualifying is split into Q1, Q2, and Q3 knockout rounds to determine the starting grid order.
  4. Track Limits & Penalties: Leaving the track to gain a lasting advantage or causing a collision results in time penalties.
